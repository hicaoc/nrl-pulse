NRL Pulse 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-09-01  |  版本 1.0

中文English

NRL Pulse（以下简称"本应用"）是一款面向业余无线电与应急通信的桌面客户端，支持 NRL2 与 FMO 两套协议的全双工语音调度。我们尊重并保护用户隐私。本政策说明本应用收集、使用与处理信息的方式。本应用不收集、不上传任何用于广告或用户画像的数据。

1. 我们收集与处理的信息

1.1 语音数据

  • 仅当您按下 PTT（发射）键时，本应用才会采集麦克风音频，并实时编码发送到您所连接的服务器/群组，用于语音通联。
  • 语音数据以实时流的形式传输，本应用不会将您的语音上传到任何与通联无关的服务器。

1.2 电台身份（呼号 / SSID）

  • 您在设置中填写的呼号与 SSID 用于在协议中标识您的身份，会随语音、文本调度消息、APRS 信令发送给对方台站或服务器。这是业余无线电通联的必要组成部分。

1.3 账号与凭据

  • 平台账号或 HAM ID 长期 Token 仅用于向您所选服务器完成身份认证，凭据只发送至对应服务器，不会发送给无关第三方。
  • FMO 模式下的证书（cert_user.json 等）由您本地导入，保存在本地，用于 SAS 认证。

1.4 设备信息（MAC 地址）

  • 当您使用"自动获取证书"功能时，本应用会读取本机 MAC 地址，并发送到证书服务（hamptt.com）以验证该设备是否已登记，从而拉取并激活证书。仅在您主动点击该功能时才会发生。

1.5 位置信息（仅当您启用信标）

  • 本应用不会自动获取或上报您的地理位置。只有当您在 FMO 进阶功能中主动开启"个人信标（BEACON）"并自行填写位置/自定义消息时，相关信息才会作为 APRS 信标发送。是否发送、发送什么内容完全由您控制。

1.6 本地配置与录音

  • 您的配置（协议、服务器、端口、呼号、音量、PTT 键、编码、串口、录音路径等）仅保存在本地。
  • "本地录音"功能开启后，语音会录制并保存到您指定的本地目录，不会上传。

2. 我们如何使用信息

  • 实现语音通联、文本调度、QSO 呼叫、房间监听、串口透传等核心功能。
  • 完成服务器身份认证与证书激活。
  • 显示连接状态、在线台站、网络质量等运行信息。

3. 信息的共享与第三方

  • 语音、呼号、文本消息会发送到您主动选择的 NRL / FMO 服务器及其上的其他台站，这是通联本身的性质决定的。
  • FMO 模式会连接 APRS-IS（如 rotate.aprs2.net）用于服务器发现；会自动连接您所选的 FMO 服务器（MQTT）。
  • 自动获取证书时会连接 hamptt.com。
  • 除上述为实现通联所必需的连接外，本应用不向任何第三方共享您的数据，不含任何广告、统计或追踪 SDK。

4. 数据安全

  • 证书与账号凭据保存在本地，请妥善保管您的设备与证书文件。
  • 语音经编码后通过 UDP / MQTT 传输；请注意业余无线电通信内容在相应网络内对成员可见。

5. 数据留存

  • 本应用不在自有服务器上留存您的语音或个人数据。
  • 本地录音、配置由您自行管理，可随时删除。

6. 您的权利

  • 您可随时在设置中修改或清除呼号、服务器、录音路径等信息。
  • 您可随时停用麦克风（不按 PTT 即不采集）、关闭录音、关闭信标。
  • 如需注销平台账号，请联系相应服务器/平台方。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用面向业余无线电爱好者，需在持有相应电台执照与呼号的前提下使用，不面向儿童收集数据。

8. 政策更新

本政策如有更新，将在本页发布并更新"生效日期"。继续使用即视为接受更新后的政策。

Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: 2026-09-01

NRL Pulse ("the App") is a desktop client for amateur radio and emergency communications, supporting full-duplex voice dispatch over the NRL2 and FMO protocols. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles information. The App does not collect or upload any data for advertising or profiling.

1. Information We Process

1.1 Voice Data

  • Audio from your microphone is captured only while you hold PTT, encoded, and streamed in real time to the server/group you connected to, solely for voice communication.
  • Voice is not uploaded to any server unrelated to the communication.

1.2 Station Identity (Callsign / SSID)

  • The callsign and SSID you configure identify you in the protocol and are sent with voice, dispatch messages, and APRS signaling to other stations/servers — a necessary part of amateur radio operation.

1.3 Account & Credentials

  • Your platform account or HAM ID token is used only to authenticate with the server you select and is sent only to that server.
  • FMO certificates you import are stored locally and used for SAS authentication.

1.4 Device Information (MAC address)

  • Only when you explicitly use "Auto-fetch certificate", the App reads your MAC address and sends it to the certificate service (hamptt.com) to verify device registration and activate the certificate.

1.5 Location (only if you enable beacons)

  • The App does not automatically collect or report your location. Only if you manually enable the personal APRS beacon and fill in location/custom text will such data be transmitted — fully under your control.

1.6 Local Config & Recordings

  • Your settings are stored locally. If you enable local recording, audio is saved to a folder you choose and is not uploaded.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide voice communication, dispatch messaging, QSO calls, room monitoring, and serial tunneling.
  • To authenticate with servers and activate certificates.
  • To display connection status, online stations, and network quality.

3. Sharing & Third Parties

  • Voice, callsign, and messages are sent to the NRL/FMO server you choose and to other stations on it — inherent to radio communication.
  • FMO mode connects to APRS-IS (e.g., rotate.aprs2.net) for server discovery and to your chosen FMO server (MQTT).
  • Auto-fetch certificate connects to hamptt.com.
  • Beyond these communication-essential connections, we do not share your data with third parties. No advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs are included.

4. Data Security

  • Certificates and credentials are stored locally; keep your device and certificate files safe.
  • Voice is transmitted over UDP/MQTT; note that amateur radio content is visible to members of the respective network.

5. Data Retention

  • We do not retain your voice or personal data on our own servers. Local recordings and settings are under your control and can be deleted anytime.

6. Your Choices

  • You may edit or clear callsign, server, and recording path in settings at any time.
  • Not pressing PTT means no audio is captured; you can disable recording and beacons at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for licensed amateur radio operators and does not collect data from children.

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date.
